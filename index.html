About us

StFX students have skills worth sharing and skills they want to learn. But paid tutoring is expensive, and informal groups can be difficult to organize.

CampusSkillSwap gives students a trusted, convenient way to connect, exchange one-hour sessions, and build useful skills together.

Student prototype for BSAD 281 at St. Francis Xavier University. Not a real company — all data is fictional.
